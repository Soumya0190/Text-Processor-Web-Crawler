Valuable Pages 
For our scraper, we are determining the value of a page by the length of text it contains. We believe text is the most valuable aspect of a page, so valuable pages for us are those pages with a high percentage of text. For SEO, Forbes indicates that an average of 600-700 words are optimal per page. Forbes also states that websites with less than 300 words per page are considered “thin” and most likely will not rank highly in search. Similarly, many other Google search results also recommended a minimum of 300 words per page. Thus, we have decided that valuable pages, for us, will have at least 300 words. 
In addition, we also calculated the percentage of stop words in a text. According to ResearchGate, a platform used by researchers and scientists to share their findings, “stop words [are] the most commonly used words in a particular language. For most URLs we found 20% to 30% of text were stop words in the text content.” Since the average of stop words in a page is around 20-30%, we figured it will be common to come across pages with anywhere from 15-35% of the text being stop words. So for our crawler we said if a page contains text, where more than 35% of the content is stop words, then it is a low value page.
